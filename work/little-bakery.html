The Little Bakery Co.

The Problem

Delicious bread, long queues

The bakery was beloved but stuck in 2010 — orders by phone or in person, daily menus posted on Facebook, and a steady stream of disappointed customers when the popular bakes sold out by midday.

  • Phone ordering created bottlenecks at peak times
  • Menus scattered across social media posts
  • No way to reserve popular bakes
  • Walk-in queues during school-run rush
The Solution

The daily menu, digitised

We built a Shopify storefront with the week's menu published every morning, pre-paid order-ahead for pickup, and automatic sell-out handling for limited bakes. Staff dashboards are one tap — no training curve.

  • Custom storefront matching their warm brand
  • Daily menu publishing with automatic sell-outs
  • Pre-paid pickups cutting checkout time to zero
  • Simple staff dashboard — two taps to fulfil
