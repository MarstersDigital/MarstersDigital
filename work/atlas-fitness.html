The Problem

Strong workouts, weak brand

Atlas had the energy but not the identity. A generic stock logo, mismatched fonts across their materials, and no website — booking enquiries happened in DMs, one at a time.

  • Stock logo with no distinctive mark
  • Inconsistent colours and fonts across channels
  • No web presence — bookings lost in Instagram DMs
  • No brand assets to hand to designers or printers
The Solution

A brand that hits hard

We designed a full identity from scratch: a bold logomark built around their name, complete logo suite, colour palette, typography system and a brand guideline kit they could hand to anyone. Then we turned it into a landing page with class schedules and instant booking.

  • Primary, secondary and mark logo variations
  • Full brand guideline kit — colours, type, usage rules
  • Vector assets in every format, print and web
  • Conversion-focused landing page with online booking
