Crestway Accountants

A dated logo was quietly costing them trust. The rebrand made Crestway look like the modern, reassuring firm they are — enquiries up 38%.

The Problem

Looked like a firm from 1998

Crestway's service was excellent, but their 1990s-era logo and mismatched stationery told a different story. Prospective clients — especially younger business owners — were choosing firms that looked more modern.

  • Outdated logo and inconsistent stationery
  • No defined colour palette or typography
  • Brand assets unusable across digital channels
  • Trust deficit with younger business owners
The Solution

Modern, measured, unmistakable

We built a clean, confident identity that signals stability without the starch: a refreshed logomark, a full colour and typography system, and a comprehensive guidelines kit so every document, deck and website stays on-brand.

  • Refreshed logomark and full logo suite
  • Colour palette and typography system
  • Templates for letters, reports and email signatures
  • Guidelines kit covering every touchpoint
