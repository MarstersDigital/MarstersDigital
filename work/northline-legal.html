The Problem

A brilliant firm, invisible online

Northline's old site was slow, dated and couldn't keep up with the firm's growth. Services were buried three clicks deep, the mobile experience was unusable, and the enquiry form regularly lost submissions.

  • 8+ second loads on mobile data
  • Practice areas unreachable from the homepage
  • Enquiry forms silently dropping submissions
  • No clear path for new clients to get advice
The Solution

Built around how clients think

We restructured the entire information architecture around client needs, not internal departments. A fast, accessible build with practice-area hubs, clear pricing guidance, and a client portal for existing clients to track their matters.

  • Custom design system matching their premium brand
  • Practice-area hubs with clear next steps on every page
  • Client portal for documents, updates and billing
  • Reliable enquiry routing with email notifications
The Tech Stack

Under the hood

Next.js Tailwind CSS Headless CMS Client Portal Edge Caching Analytics

A static-first Next.js build on a headless CMS means edits are instant and the site stays fast as it grows. Managed care plan included.
