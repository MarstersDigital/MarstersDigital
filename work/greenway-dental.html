The Problem

New patients couldn't find them

Greenway's website was built by a template mill: dated imagery, no booking options, and a contact page that only had a phone number. New patients either rang during peak hours or gave up.

  • Template site indistinguishable from competitors
  • No online booking — phone lines overwhelmed
  • Little local search visibility for key treatments
  • No clear pricing or treatment information
The Solution

Designed to reduce anxiety, built to convert

We rebuilt the site around the two things patients care about: feeling informed and booking easily. Treatment pages with honest pricing guidance, a gentle visual tone, and online booking integrated into the practice's diary system.

  • Custom design with a calm, clinical-but-warm feel
  • Online booking wired straight into the practice diary
  • Treatment pages targeting local search terms
  • Semantic, accessible, lightning-fast build
