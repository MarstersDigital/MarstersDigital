Harbour & Co. Coffee

The Problem

A beautiful brand, a leaky store

Harbour & Co. had a strong local following and a gorgeous logo — but a slow, template-based store that confused customers. Cart abandonment was high, subscriptions were managed by spreadsheet, and stock numbers were never right.

  • Page loads of 6+ seconds on mobile
  • 68% cart abandonment on the checkout funnel
  • Subscriptions tracked manually — frequent billing errors
  • Sold-out coffees still shown for sale
The Solution

Rebuilt from the roast up

We designed a bespoke theme around their brand — warm, tactile and fast — and wired in a subscription engine that handles billing and deliveries automatically. Stock now syncs in real time from the roastery's inventory system, so what customers see is what's actually available.

  • Custom Shopify theme — designed, not templated
  • Automated subscription billing with flexible delivery dates
  • Live stock sync with the roastery's inventory system
  • One-page product storytelling that sells the roast, not just the bag
The Tech Stack

Under the hood

Shopify Plus Liquid Subscription API Inventory API CDN + Edge Caching Analytics & A/B Testing

Hosted on Shopify's infrastructure with edge caching for near-instant loads. We also moved the site onto a managed care plan — monthly edits, monitoring and backups included.
