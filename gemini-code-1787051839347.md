# Technical Specification: Marsters Digital Website

## 1. Project Overview & Brand Value Propositions
A multi-page agency website for **Marsters Digital** showcasing bespoke web development, branding, managed hosting/support, and IT consulting services. The site prioritizes high performance, modern dark-mode visuals, responsive layouts, streamlined lead capture, and relationship-first messaging.

### Core Value Propositions (To be emphasized site-wide)
* **100% Bespoke Builds**: Tailored solutions built around client goals—no rigid, bloated off-the-shelf templates.
* **Dedicated Account Manager**: Direct, 1-on-1 contact with your dedicated account manager from day one—a single point of contact with no call centre queues or endless ticket handoffs.
* **Relationship-First & Transparent**: Focus on long-term client partnerships with zero hidden fees or surprise costs.

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Design Tokens

* **Background Base**: `#0B0F14` (Deep Charcoal / Obsidian)
* **Surface / Cards**: `#121820` with subtle border `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)`
* **Primary Accent**: `#14B8A6` to `#38BDF8` (Cyan / Teal gradient)
* **Secondary Accent**: `#2563EB` to `#3B82F6` (Royal / Electric Blue)
* **Typography**:
  * Headings: `#F8FAFC` (Off-white, Sans-serif)
  * Body: `#94A3B8` (Slate Gray)
* **Design Accents**: Glassmorphism surfaces, subtle ambient glow effects, crisp 1px borders.

---

## 3. Site Architecture & Page Content

1. **Home (`/`)**:
   * **Hero**: Dynamic headline emphasizing bespoke digital solutions and direct 1-on-1 collaboration. Primary CTA leading to `/contact`.
   * **Trust / Values Banner**: 3 highlight badges: *Bespoke Code & Design*, *Dedicated Account Manager*, *Zero Hidden Fees*.
   * **Services Overview**: Quick snapshot of Web, Branding, Managed Care, and IT Consulting.
   * **Featured Work**: Visual showcase of live client builds with device mockups.
   * **The "Why Us" Section**: Focus on transparent pricing and building lasting business partnerships.

2. **Services (`/services`)**:
   * **Bespoke Web Design & Development**: 1-page landing pages, multi-page business platforms, and custom Shopify / e-commerce stores engineered for speed and conversion.
   * **Brand & Logo Identity**: Logo suites, vector assets, colour palettes, typography, and brand guideline kits.
   * **Managed Hosting & Website Care**: Fast cloud hosting, SSL, security patches, backups, and 30 minutes of monthly client edits included.
   * **IT & Technical Consulting**: Business email setups, domain management, software migrations, and technical troubleshooting.

3. **Portfolio (`/work`)**:
   * Filterable project gallery with dedicated case study template pages (`/work/[slug]`) highlighting the problem, custom solution, and tech stack.

4. **About (`/about`)**:
   * The Marsters Digital philosophy: dedicated client care, seamless account management, clear communication, and ongoing technical support.

5. **Contact (`/contact`)**:
   * High-conversion layout featuring a side-by-side (desktop) / stacked (mobile) layout pairing social proof reviews with the inquiry form.

---

## 4. Contact Page & Social Proof Specification

### Page Layout Structure
* **Desktop**: 2-column split.
  * **Left Column (Social Proof & Trust)**: Compelling copy reaffirming your dedicated account manager as the single point of contact, 2–3 client review cards with 5-star badges, and direct contact details (email/phone).
  * **Right Column (Form Card)**: Clean, high-contrast form container using a subtle glassmorphic card backdrop.
* **Mobile**: Single-column layout placing a short trust snippet and top review before the form, with additional review cards positioned beneath.

### Review Card Component Specs
* **Visual Elements**:
  * 5-star rating icons highlighted in Cyan (`#38BDF8`).
  * Direct client quote emphasizing communication with their account manager, bespoke quality, turnaround time, or lack of hidden fees.
  * Reviewer Name, Job Title / Business Name, and optional project badge (e.g., `Shopify Build`, `Logo Design`).
  * Subtle card border hover transition (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`).

## 5. Contact Form Specification

### Form Fields & Validation

| Field Name | Type | Required | Options / Validation Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Full Name** | `text` | Yes | Min 2 characters |
| **Business Name** | `text` | No | Optional |
| **Email Address** | `email` | Yes | Valid email format |
| **Phone Number** | `tel` | No | Optional UK/International format |
| **Service Required** | `select` / `chips` | Yes | Multi-select / Dropdown: <br>• Bespoke Web Development<br>• Shopify / E-Commerce<br>• Logo & Brand Identity<br>• Managed Hosting & Support Plan<br>• IT Consulting<br>• Other |
| **Estimated Timeline** | `select` | No | • Immediately<br>• Within 1 month<br>• 1–3 months<br>• Flexible |
| **Project Details** | `textarea` | Yes | Min 20 characters; outline goals, current website (if applicable), and requirements |

### Functional & UX Requirements
* **Spam Protection**: Cloudflare Turnstile or invisible reCAPTCHA v3 plus a honeypot field.
* **Submission Handling**: Serverless API route triggering automated email notifications via Resend, Postmark, or SendGrid.
* **Form Feedback States**:
  * Default & Hover/Focus state with cyan glow border (`#38BDF8`).
  * Submitting spinner state.
  * Success notification: *"Thanks for reaching out! Your account manager will be in touch within 24 hours."*
  * Error fallback banner with a direct mailto link.

---

## 6. Technical Stack & Non-Functional Requirements

* **Framework**: Next.js (App Router) or Astro for static speed, SEO, and developer ergonomics.
* **Styling**: Tailwind CSS.
* **SEO & Metadata**: Dynamic OpenGraph image generation, schema markup for local services/reviews, clean semantic HTML5, and a target 95+ score on Google Lighthouse.
